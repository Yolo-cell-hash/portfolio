04 · Experience

Assistant Manager, Product Design Engineer

Godrej & Boyce · Mumbai

Aug 2024 — Present

I work on the software side of Godrej's smart products: the apps and SDKs that let a phone or tablet find a device, talk to it securely and control it, plus the prototypes that show product teams what's possible. I introduced Flutter here so one codebase could serve Android, iOS and iPad.

Smart digital locks

  • A phone app for fingerprint locks. For Godrej's digital rim lock and smart padlock: find nearby locks, lock and unlock over Bluetooth, add or remove fingerprints and set auto-lock. You can also lock or unlock over the internet when you're away.
  • Longer battery, same speed. I reworked how the Bluetooth SDK scans, so the phone listens only for its own locks. Battery life went up 180%, with no slower response.
  • Building blocks for other apps. Bluetooth SDKs for Android and iOS that other Godrej apps use to talk to locks, tuned for locks that broadcast every 1.5 seconds.
  • Private conversations. Encrypted pairing, using an ECC key exchange plus AES, so nobody nearby can read what the phone tells the lock.
  • Hands-free unlock. The door opens as you walk up, with the phone still in your pocket. It's now a published patent.
  • Where's my bag? A companion app for the e-MyLock Candy smart luggage lock: live location on a map, a safe zone you draw, and an alert the moment the bag leaves it.

Video door bells

  • See who's at the door, from anywhere. The app for Godrej's video doorbell: live video, talk back, and unlock the door remotely.
  • Near-instant video. About 20 ms of delay using WebRTC, as smooth as a video call, whether you're at home or across the internet.
  • Rings like a phone call. A press of the bell shows a full-screen incoming call, like WhatsApp, even when the app is closed.
  • Set-up without typing on the device. The phone hands a new doorbell your home Wi-Fi details over Bluetooth.
  • Knows your family. Register faces in the app, and a face model running on the device checks visitors, with anti-spoofing so a photo won't fool it.
  • Ready to plug in. Packaged as an SDK that the native Android team adds through a small Kotlin API. It also plays recordings straight off the doorbell's SD card over home Wi-Fi.
  • Across hardware. Worked with doorbell boards built on Raspberry Pi and Realtek chips, and designed the cloud link on AWS IoT Core.

Home automation & platform

  • Godrej ONE. The single app for every Godrej product in your home. See it above.
  • Godrej HOME. An iPad control panel for the whole house, with a 3D view of every room.
  • Flutter, everywhere. Introduced Flutter as the shared framework for prototypes across product teams, with one codebase for Android, iOS and iPad.
  • Cameras from other brands. Integrated third-party IP camera SDKs natively, in Kotlin on Android and Objective-C on iOS.
  • Advantis IoT module. A Flutter module dropped into the existing Advantis Android app to monitor devices live through Firebase.
